What's new in 0.11.9

- On a bad connection, a session that stopped responding is noticed within seconds of typing and reconnects, instead of over a minute later.
- Reconnects keep trying when the server is slow to answer or drops the connection before logging in, instead of giving up.
- Closing a Mosh session stops the server's mosh-server again on servers like Alpine.
- Fewer false crash reports from reconnects in the background.
