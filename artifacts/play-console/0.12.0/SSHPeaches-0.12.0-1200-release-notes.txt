What's new in 0.12.0

- New Boost (Settings > Boost): faster browsing and transfers, and terminals that survive network changes like Mosh, over UDP via a helper the app starts on your Linux server.
- Much faster transfers: chunks over several connections, faster encryption.
- Transfers survive dropped or changing networks and resume.
- Browse, rename and edit while files transfer; more transfers queue up.
- The file browser reconnects by itself.
- Big folders and links open much faster.
