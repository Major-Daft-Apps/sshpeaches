What's new in 0.14.1

- Fixed an occasional crash after cancelling a Boost download.
- Old SSH algorithms (SHA-1, CBC) are now off by default, as in OpenSSH. Turn them on for an old server in that host's settings.
- Text copied from the terminal is cleared from the clipboard after a minute.
- Stronger encryption for exported passwords and keys.
- Security updates to the built-in Mosh client.
