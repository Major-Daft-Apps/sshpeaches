Welcome to SSHPeaches 1.0, a free and open-source SSH client for Android.

- SSH terminal with extra keys (Ctrl, Esc, Tab, arrows, F1-F12) and snippets
- SFTP file browser: upload, download and edit files on your server
- Mosh and Boost keep sessions alive on bad or changing networks
- Password and SSH key login, with keys generated on your phone
- Port forwarding, QR sharing between phones, and an app lock

No ads, no subscriptions.
