What's new in 0.11.7

- 10 new flat app icons in Settings. Orange on cream replaces the 3D icon.
- Themed icons show the peach's segments, not a white curve.
- After a reconnect, tmux no longer pushes the screen up a line every second.
- Reconnects retry instead of failing with "Not connected".
- The connection log reads like ssh -vv. Passwords and terminal data are never logged.
- Importing a saved host no longer resets its OS icon.
- Widget: open connections show just user@host.
