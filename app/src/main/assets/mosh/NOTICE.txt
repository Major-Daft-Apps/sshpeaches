The bundled mosh-client is built from source by tools/mosh-client/build.sh:
- mosh 1.4.0 (GPL-3.0-or-later, with an OpenSSL linking exception) https://mosh.org
- protobuf 36.2, lite runtime (BSD-3-Clause) https://github.com/protocolbuffers/protobuf
- Abseil 20250512.1, the parts protobuf uses (Apache-2.0) https://abseil.io
- OpenSSL 3.5.9, libcrypto (Apache-2.0) https://www.openssl.org
- ncurses 6.5 and its terminfo entries (MIT-style X11 license) https://invisible-island.net/ncurses/
- libc++ from the Android NDK (Apache-2.0 with LLVM exceptions)
